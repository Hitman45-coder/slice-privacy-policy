Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Slice is a puzzle game that shows advertisements. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices around advertising. We do not collect or store any personal information about you.

1. Information We Collect

Slice itself does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no in-app purchases. Your high score, audio settings, and tutorial progress are saved only in the app's local storage on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. When ads are shown, AdMob (and its advertising partners) may process the following to select and deliver ads:

  • Your device's advertising ID and device identifiers;
  • Approximate location, where you have granted permission;
  • Technical information such as device model, OS version, and IP address;
  • Cookies or similar technologies, where applicable.

AdMob may use this information to show personalized ads based on your interests. You can view, change, or reset your ad preferences at any time in Google's Ad Settings, and you can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's advertising settings.

3. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through the same consent screen.

4. Children

Slice is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly request or collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet — required to load and display ads;
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback while playing.

6. Third-Party Services

AdMob is operated by Google LLC. Google's own privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the practices of advertising partners or other third parties.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and shown inside the app.
